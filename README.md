# My Favorite Anime: Demon Slayer

*Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* is my favorite anime. It's based on the manga by Koyoharu Gotouge and animated by *Ufotable*.

## Story Summary

Tanjiro Kamado is a kind-hearted boy whose family is killed by demons. His younger sister, Nezuko, survives but turns into a demon. Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps to find a way to turn her back into a human.

## Main Characters

| Character | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Tanjiro Kamado | Main character, uses Water Breathing |
| Nezuko Kamado | Tanjiro's sister, a demon who protects humans |
| Zenitsu Agatsuma | Demon slayer, uses Thunder Breathing |
